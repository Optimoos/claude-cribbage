Cribbage desktop client, version @VERSION@

Start it:
  Windows: double-click Cribbage.exe. Windows may say "Windows protected
    your PC", because this app is not signed: choose "More info", then
    "Run anyway".
  Linux: run ./Cribbage.x86_64 (if it will not start, chmod +x it first).

Keep every file in this folder together: the app needs them all.

Sign in with your email address: we email you a code. A new address
creates an account. The app connects to @SERVER@
(you can change it on the sign-in screen).

When the server says the app is out of date, choose "Updates" on the
sign-in screen to download the new version.

Report a bug: https://github.com/Optimoos/claude-cribbage/issues
